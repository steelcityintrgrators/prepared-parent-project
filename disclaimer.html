Educational information. Not legal advice.

The Prepared Parent Project is not a law firm.

The Prepared Parent Project does not provide legal advice, legal representation, or attorney services. No use of this website, resource navigator, educational material, social-media content, email, form, or program creates an attorney-client relationship.

Information may discuss Alabama statutes, court rules, published Alabama case law, official agency materials, court forms, and other public resources for educational purposes. Laws and procedures can change, and different facts can lead to different legal issues or outcomes.

Parents should consult a licensed attorney for advice about their individual rights, deadlines, filings, strategy, or representation. When appropriate, Prepared Parent may identify independent legal-aid or attorney referral resources, but those providers make their own eligibility and representation decisions.

Case-law and source policy

Legal-content items remain behind a verification gate until the applicable authority has been reviewed and recorded. Missing or unverified authority is a reason not to publish a legal proposition as verified.

Emergency and safety issues

This website is not an emergency service. Immediate danger, suspected child abuse or neglect, domestic violence, or other urgent safety issues require appropriate emergency, child-protection, domestic-violence, or licensed professional resources.
