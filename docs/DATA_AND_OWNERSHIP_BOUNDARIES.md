# Data and Ownership Boundaries

## Current custody
The Prepared Parent Project is still being organized and has not been represented here as a completed nonprofit filing. Ronald Williams is the current project custodian. Formation documents, board action, and professional review must determine the final legal owner of intellectual property and accounts.

## Mandatory separation
The project must remain separate from:
- Steel City Integrators LLC and Media Tek Pros;
- Kratos Techne Holding Company;
- AMW Family Trust;
- ChurchReach AI, Church Live Network, and ClearSpeak AI;
- Ronald Williams's private custody and child-support case files.

## Public repository content
Only public educational materials, source-verified Alabama information, public website code, and sanitized examples belong here. Private parent submissions, donor records, board-confidential material, banking information, and case-specific evidence belong in restricted nonprofit-controlled storage.

## Content controls
All legal or court-related educational material must:
1. identify applicable Alabama authority;
2. distinguish verified law from practical guidance;
3. avoid one-size-fits-all conclusions;
4. state that the project is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice.

## Contributors
Written agreements should address confidentiality, intellectual-property assignment or licensing, reuse rights, and removal of third-party confidential information.
