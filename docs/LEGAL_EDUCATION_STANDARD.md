# Situation-Aware Legal Education Standard

Effective: October 21, 2026

## Identity and scope
The Prepared Parent Project is not a law firm. It does not provide legal advice, legal representation, or create an attorney-client relationship.

## Mandatory disclaimer
The Prepared Parent Project is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice or legal representation. Information provided through the website, social media, educational materials, programs, and technology is for general educational and informational purposes only. Laws and court procedures can change, and every family's circumstances are different. Information about Alabama law may include statutes, court rules, and published Alabama case law. Nothing provided creates an attorney-client relationship or substitutes for advice from a licensed attorney regarding an individual situation.

## Publication gate for Alabama legal content
For any public content that explains Alabama legal rights, duties, standards, court procedures, custody, child support, DHR enforcement, modification, contempt, evidence, or relief:

1. Identify the exact legal proposition.
2. Identify the relevant fact pattern and procedural posture.
3. Verify the applicable Alabama statute and/or court rule.
4. Verify relevant published Alabama appellate or Supreme Court authority when case law is applicable.
5. Record case name, citation, court, year, source URL, pinpoint support, and treatment/current status.
6. Separate the actual holding/rule from the project's plain-language explanation.
7. Identify material factual distinctions, exceptions, uncertainty, and procedural differences.
8. Require human legal/safety review for high-risk legal content.
9. Never treat AI output, a secondary website, or a social-media post as sufficient legal verification.
10. Recheck time-sensitive sources immediately before publication.

Missing or unverified authority is a stop condition.

## Situation-aware intake
Before presenting potentially applicable legal authorities, collect only the facts reasonably necessary to identify the educational issue. Depending on topic, these can include:
- whether a written order exists;
- initial case vs modification vs enforcement/contempt vs appeal vs DHR/administrative matter;
- current custody/parenting arrangement;
- county/court and procedural stage;
- what changed and when;
- child-support or enforcement status;
- the outcome the parent is trying to understand;
- urgent safety, domestic violence, child protection, incarceration, military, interstate, or other special circumstances.

## Prohibited positioning
Do not use language that reasonably implies legal representation, including:
- law firm
- our attorneys
- our legal team
- legal consultation
- case evaluation
- your legal strategy
- we represent you

Preferred framing includes Parent Resource Center, Alabama Family Law Education, Court Preparation Resources, Case-Law Library, Parent Education, Resource Navigator, and Find Legal Help.

## AI rule
AI may assist with issue spotting, source organization, research comparison, drafting, content repurposing, and routing. AI must not invent citations, holdings, quotes, deadlines, procedural requirements, or outcomes.
