# Prepared Parent Legal Search & Verification Standard

## Purpose
Every parent-facing search, navigator result, educational answer, content item, and resource recommendation that touches Alabama law must be traceable to verified Alabama legal authority and the facts/procedural posture that make the authority relevant.

The Prepared Parent Project is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice or representation. This standard governs educational legal information only.

## Required verification stack
Before a legal answer can be labeled VERIFIED, the system must identify and record all applicable layers:

1. **Issue identification**
   - Exact legal question.
   - Parent's factual scenario.
   - Procedural posture: initial case, modification, enforcement/contempt, appeal, DHR/administrative, post-judgment, etc.
   - Existing order status.
   - Court/county where relevant.
   - Special circumstances such as interstate jurisdiction, domestic violence, military status, incarceration, paternity, self-employment, or child safety.

2. **Controlling Alabama authority**
   - Applicable Code of Alabama provision(s).
   - Applicable Alabama Rules of Judicial Administration, Alabama Rules of Civil Procedure, Alabama Rules of Appellate Procedure, Alabama Rules of Evidence, juvenile rules, or other court rules when relevant.
   - Official Alabama Judicial System forms and instructions when procedure/forms are discussed.

3. **Published Alabama case law**
   - Relevant Alabama Supreme Court and/or Alabama Court of Civil Appeals decisions.
   - Case name.
   - Reporter citation or official citation.
   - Court and year.
   - Source URL.
   - Pinpoint page/paragraph or quoted language supporting the proposition.
   - Factual similarity and material distinctions.
   - Procedural posture.
   - Current treatment: followed, distinguished, limited, overruled, superseded by rule/statute, or unknown.
   - Date last verified.

4. **Agency / administrative authority**
   - Alabama DHR or other official agency source when the question involves agency programs, enforcement, benefits, or administrative procedure.
   - Federal authority when federal law controls or materially affects the Alabama process.

5. **Conflict and hierarchy check**
   - Statute/rule changes after the case.
   - Later Alabama appellate cases affecting the holding.
   - Conflicts between older and newer authority.
   - Whether the cited case is factually distinguishable.
   - Whether the case is published/precedential.
   - Whether the proposition comes from a holding rather than dicta.

6. **Plain-language explanation**
   - Separate the source rule/holding from Prepared Parent's explanation.
   - Explain what facts matter.
   - Explain exceptions and uncertainty.
   - Never state a likely court outcome as guaranteed.
   - Never turn general educational information into individualized legal advice.

## Answer status labels
Every legal search result must display one of these statuses:

- **VERIFIED — ALABAMA AUTHORITY COMPLETE**
  Statute/rule + relevant published case law + current-treatment check + source links + human review complete.

- **VERIFIED — PRIMARY SOURCE ONLY**
  Appropriate only where a legal proposition does not reasonably require case-law interpretation, such as locating an official form or quoting a current agency application process.

- **CASE-LAW REVIEW REQUIRED**
  Statute/rule or agency source exists, but relevant appellate authority has not yet been verified.

- **CONFLICT / DISTINCTION REVIEW REQUIRED**
  Authorities may conflict or the user's facts materially differ from the source cases.

- **UNVERIFIED — DO NOT RELY ON THIS AS LEGAL INFORMATION**
  The system does not yet have enough verified authority to provide a legal explanation.

## Hard-stop rule
The site must not generate a definitive Alabama legal answer merely because a keyword matched a statute, rule, DHR page, prior content item, AI response, or generic web summary.

If the required Alabama legal authority has not been verified, the public response must stop at:
- what issue appears to be involved;
- what facts may matter;
- what official sources are known;
- what authority is still missing;
- where to seek licensed legal help.

## Search-result evidence packet
Each verified answer should be capable of displaying or exporting:

- User question (redacted of unnecessary personal information)
- Legal issue(s)
- Facts used in issue matching
- Procedural posture
- Statute(s)
- Rule(s)
- Case(s)
- Case citation(s)
- Pinpoint support
- Source links
- Agency source(s)
- Current-treatment check
- Distinctions/exceptions
- Verification date
- Reviewer status
- Educational explanation
- Disclaimer

## Citation integrity
The system must never fabricate:
- case names;
- reporter citations;
- docket numbers;
- quotations;
- holdings;
- pinpoint citations;
- dates;
- procedural history;
- statutory text;
- rule text;
- deadlines.

Missing information is a stop condition, not an invitation to infer or invent it.
