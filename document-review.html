Document Review Beta

Understand what you received. Prepare what to verify next.

Select court papers, service documents, DHR notices, orders, motions, or related records. The current privacy-first beta processes file names and plain-text content locally in your browser and never uploads your files to a server.

Important legal limit: this tool does not tell you what you are legally required to file, calculate deadlines, or replace a lawyer. It identifies document signals, possible filing categories to research, questions to verify with the court or a licensed attorney, and items you may want to organize for court.

1. Add your documents

Privacy: files selected here stay in this browser. Plain-text files can be read locally. PDF, DOC/DOCX, and image contents are not transmitted or silently uploaded.

2. Your preparation report

Add documents or paste key text, then choose Review my documents.

Fail-closed rule: if the tool cannot reliably identify a filing or deadline, it will say so instead of inventing one.

What the secure AI version will add

Full document intelligence without exposing legal documents publicly.

The next backend phase will support secure PDF/DOCX/image extraction, document classification, deadline detection, Alabama-source matching, filing-category suggestions, bring-to-court checklists, and attorney-review escalation. No automatic filing or legal representation.
