Legal-content publication gate

Each legal proposition should record the issue, facts that matter, statute or rule, relevant Alabama case authority, source link, pinpoint support, date verified, distinctions, and review status.

Stop condition: if required case-law support has not been verified, the content remains unpublished. About the project

Education, preparation, and resource navigation for Alabama parents.

Mission direction

Preparing Alabama parents. Strengthening Alabama families. The project is designed to help parents understand processes, organize information, locate reliable resources, and better prepare for decisions affecting their families.

Source and review standards

Legal and court-related content should distinguish primary law, official agency guidance, verified Alabama appellate authority, and plain-language educational explanation. AI-generated citations or unsupported legal propositions are not acceptable publication sources.

Not a law firm. Not legal advice.

The Prepared Parent Project does not provide legal advice, legal representation, or attorney services. No use of this website, resource navigator, educational material, social-media content, email, form, or program creates an attorney-client relationship. Parents should consult a licensed attorney for advice about individual rights, deadlines, filings, strategy, or representation.
